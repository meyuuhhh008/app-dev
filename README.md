### *The Chosen*  

**Genre:** Drama, Faith-based  
**Director:** Dallas Jenkins  
**Streaming Platform:** Netflix  

> “Come and see.”  
> — *Jesus (The Chosen, Season 1)*  

#### Why I Love This Series  
- It brings Bible stories to life with depth and humanity.  
- Every episode makes you feel connected to Jesus and His disciples.  
- The cinematography and music are just *chef’s kiss*  

#### Favorite Scene  
When Jesus meets Mary Magdalene after she’s freed from darkness — the way He calls her by name shows how deeply He knows and loves us.  

#### Rating  
(5/5) — highly recommended for anyone seeking inspiration.
